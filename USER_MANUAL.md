# Notif Tiles – User Manual

Notif Tiles is a home-screen widget that shows your notifications as tiles, with a clock, weather, fast-dial contacts, app shortcuts, quick notes, a voice recorder and a "Where to?" button for Google Maps.

## 1. First-time setup
1. Install the APK (GitHub → Releases → latest build). Builds are signed with the same key, so a new build installs over the old one.
2. Open **Notif Tiles setup** and turn on **Notification access** (the status line shows "on" or "OFF").
3. Choose which apps appear as tiles.
4. Add the widget: long-press the home screen → Widgets → Notif Tiles.
5. When asked, allow the app to run without battery limits. Some phones stop background apps, which freezes the widget.

## 2. What the widget shows
| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Clock, weather, date | Tap the clock or date to open the clock or calendar. Weather comes from Open-Meteo. |
| Where to? | Opens a destination box for Google Maps. During navigation it becomes "Exit navigation". |
| Fast-dial circles | Up to 5 contacts. Tap to call. |
| Shortcuts row | Up to 5 app or link shortcuts. |
| Inbox row | Title, mic (voice recorder), + Note, Clear all, pencil ✎, settings gear. |
| Message tiles | Your notifications. Tap a tile to open it or reply. |
| Refresh icon and "Updated …" line | Refreshes the weather and shows when it was last updated. |

## 3. The pencil ✎ menu
Tap the pencil on the widget. It has these options:

### Move
Press and hold any item and drag it anywhere. It stays exactly where you drop it (Free layout).
- While dragging, an item clicks into line with its neighbours (same height, same left or right edge) when it is close.
- **Tidy up: align items in each row** puts items of a row on one line.
- **Tidy up: align and space evenly** also spaces the items of a row equally.
- Drag the bar **Messages start here** to choose where the message tiles begin.
- **Back to the old rows layout** forgets all places and returns to the normal rows.
- Places are saved for your widget width and stretch sideways if you resize the widget.

### Arrange & resize
- Section order and section sizes (rows layout).
- For every item: **Size** (scales the item and its text), **Shape width only** and **Shape height only** (stretch the shape, the text stays the same size), colour, and a show/hide switch.
- **Where to? box width**: far left means it fills the row; otherwise a fixed width.
- **Merge into one shape**: show clock, weather and date together in one shape, one under the other, or side by side with the "Side by side" option.

### Saved layouts
Save the widget's look and places under a name (for example "Work"), load it later, or delete it. Loading replaces the current look and places.

### Reset
Puts the widget back to the standard layout: top row, fast-dial circles, shortcuts row, Inbox row, pencil shown, refresh icon and weather line. It removes all themes, colours, fonts, sizes and moved places. Your apps, contacts, shortcuts and notes are kept.

### Undo last reset or load
Appears after a Reset or a loaded layout. It brings back the look and places you had before. Custom background pictures are not brought back.

## 4. Look & colors (gear)
Sections: Themes & accent colour, Text, fonts & colours, Shapes & borders, Backgrounds & pictures, Show / hide parts, Widget (clock, weather & layout), Fast dial, Shortcuts row, Buttons (+ Note, Clear all, Settings), Counts & clearing, Pages, shortcuts & grouping, Apps (pin, mute, colour), Quiet hours & snooze, Notes, Voice recorder, Where to? (maps & location), Backup & restore.

## 5. Backup & restore
Backup & restore (in Look & colors) exports every setting and the background pictures as one file, and imports it again. Keep a copy before big changes. Saved layouts are part of the backup.

## 6. Tips
- Hidden items are shown again in Arrange & resize first, then they can be moved.
- If the widget looks crooked after moving items, use **Tidy up** in Move.
- If notifications stop arriving, open the app once: it reconnects the notification service.

## 7. Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Widget empty or frozen | Open Notif Tiles setup, check Notification access is on, allow running without battery limits. |
| "Where to?" fills the whole row | Arrange & resize → Where to? box → Box width. |
| Items overlap after resizing the widget | Open Move and use Tidy up, or drag the items again. |
| Everything is messy | Pencil → Reset (you can undo it). |
